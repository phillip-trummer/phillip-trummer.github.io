This is a placeholder post. A first post can be modest: why the site exists, what you expect to
write about, and what kind of people you hope will find it useful.

The design should stay out of the way. The page can carry a little personality through rhythm,
spacing, links, and the choice of what to keep.

## A useful shape

- Short updates that are easier to publish than essays.
- Longer writeups when an idea keeps returning.
- Photos or notes from places that shaped the work.
